The AMAR Operational Workflow

A seamless, deterministic progression from incident reporting to finalized procurement.

01

Scan Asset QR

Staff scans the equipment QR code via mobile. Ticket data is instantly auto-populated.

02

AI Interception

The integrated LLM provides instant, manual-based troubleshooting to resolve minor issues.

03

Admin Verification

If unresolved, the Department Admin verifies the breakdown and assesses repair requirements.

04

Procurement Sync

Unrepairable assets are pushed to the Purchase Officer, bridging maintenance and acquisition.
